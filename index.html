About

Counterrevolutionary Concepts is an independent creative development studio based in Australia, dedicated to cultural renewal through traditional principles. We create digital tools, written content, and educational resources that reconnect individuals and communities with natural rhythm, historical insight, and spiritual structure.

Current Project

Our flagship product, Roman Solar Time, is an iOS application that revives the ancient method of solar-based timekeeping. It is expected to release in November 2025, sunlight permitting.

Roman Solar Time bridges the divide between the mechanical precision that early clockmakers sought to provide and the traditional, solar-based timekeeping practiced since antiquity.
